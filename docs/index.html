About

For more than a decade I have built and run post-sales organizations at technology companies, from the first hire to global teams, and through acquisitions by Amazon and VMware.

I joined CloudHealth Technologies in 2015 as its first Technical Account Manager, when the company was between its seed round and Series A. Over seven years I built the Customer Success function, led it through the acquisition by VMware, and finished as Global Head of Customer Success with a portfolio above $200M in ARR. Since then I have led Customer Success at Exabeam and Abnormal AI, and today I am Chief Customer Officer at Onclusive.

Before software I was an engineer and researcher. My doctorate is in modeling and simulation, and my first job after it was at Kiva Systems, the warehouse-robotics company that became Amazon Robotics. That training is why I treat Customer Success as a system to be modeled and measured. Health scores, renewal forecasts and coverage models come before opinions.

Alongside operating roles I advise early-stage founders. I have also taught graduate courses in simulation and data management, and published research on traffic simulation and intelligent transportation systems. See the research.
